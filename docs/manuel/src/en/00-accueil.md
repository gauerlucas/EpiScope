# Welcome to EpiScope

!!! bref
    - **Who is it for?** Neurologists, neurophysiologists, neurosurgeons and neuropsychologists who prepare or follow a presurgical evaluation.
    - **What for?** Keeping the patient's record from the first video-EEG to post-surgical follow-up, and presenting it at the MDT meeting without building slides.
    - **How long to get started?** Ten minutes with the [quick start](@demarrage).

## EpiScope in one minute

You open one file, `EpiScope.html`, in Edge or Chrome. You choose a folder for the patient records: your own disk, or a shared team drive. From then on, everything happens in the patient's record:

1. **You describe the seizures** sign by sign, while watching the video. EpiScope shows live, on a 3D brain, the regions that can produce each sign and how the discharge may have spread.
2. **You add what you have**: EEG reports, EDF recordings, MRI and PET, neuropsychology, screenshots. Nothing is mandatory.
3. **You present the record at the MDT meeting**: EpiScope builds the slideshow (history, seizures, EEG, imaging, SEEG, concordance, hypotheses) and the report.
4. **For an SEEG**, you will find the implantation scheme, the actual electrodes on the CT, the recordings and the epileptogenicity index, stimulations, thermocoagulations and the report.

!!! astuce
    You do not need to use everything. Many colleagues start with seizure entry alone, then the MDT presentation. The rest will come when you need it.

## Before you start

**Everything belongs to a patient.** There is no seizure "without a patient": you always open a record first.

**The pathway reads from left to right.** At the top of the screen, the patient's stages appear in chronological order: *Record · Phase 1 · MDT · SEEG · MDT · Surgery · Follow-up*. Click a stage to open it; the [[+]] button adds one.

**Everything is saved automatically.** The dot next to the [[Report]] button turns green when the record is saved. There is no "Save" button to look for.

**Help is on every screen.** The [[?]] button in the top bar, or the <kbd>F1</kbd> key, opens this manual at the page for the screen you are on, in a small window you can move, enlarge or dock to the right.

![The manual opens on top of the application, at the page for the current screen. It can be moved, docked to the right or shown full screen.](shot:aide-compagnon)

**Nothing leaves your computer.** EpiScope works offline. Records are ordinary files in the folder you chose: they are backed up and shared like the rest of your documents.

!!! attention
    EpiScope is a research tool: it is not a medical device (no CE mark) and has not been validated for clinical use. It is used to document and share anatomo-electro-clinical reasoning; it does not make a diagnosis, does not localise the epileptogenic zone, and its results must not be used on their own for a diagnostic or therapeutic decision.
