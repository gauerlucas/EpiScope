# I want to…

## Everyday tasks

<div class="cards" markdown>

- [**Find a patient** Patient list (<kbd>F9</kbd>), search by pseudonym or by stage.](@patients#la-liste-des-patients)
- [**Create a patient** [[+ New patient]], a pseudonym, that is all.](@patients#creer-un-patient)
- [**Complete the clinical history** Past history, family tree, epilepsy history, treatments, development.](@patients#lhistoire-clinique)
- [**File a dated investigation** MRI, PET, neuropsychology, with attachments and screenshots.](@patients#les-bilans)
- [**Paste a screenshot** <kbd>Win</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>, then <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> in a gallery.](@patients#la-galerie)
- [**Send a record to a colleague** `.episcope` archive, full or light.](@rapports#partager-un-dossier)

</div>

## In phase 1

<div class="cards" markdown>

- [**Describe a seizure from the video** Stopwatch, sign palette, timeline.](@phase1#decrire-une-crise)
- [**Correct a sign** Drag on the timeline, right-click for the side, double-click for details.](@phase1#corriger-la-sequence)
- [**See where the seizure starts** Network at t, Origin, Targets, Model tabs.](@phase1#lire-le-resultat)
- [**Write the EEG report** Standard sentences, 10-20 map, suggested texts.](@phase1#lintercritique)
- [**Review an EDF recording** Viewer: montages, filters, gain, markers.](@phase1#relire-un-trace)
- [**Detect spikes and find their source** SpikeNet, clusters, source imaging.](@phase1#pointes-et-imagerie-de-source)
- [**Stage sleep** Automatic hypnogram, epoch-by-epoch review.](@phase1#le-sommeil)

</div>

## Imaging

<div class="cards" markdown>

- [**Import an MRI or a PET** NIfTI or a DICOM folder, dropped on the screen.](@imagerie#importer-des-images)
- [**See the patient's brain in 3D** Patient anatomy: segmentation and parcellation, in about a minute.](@imagerie#lanatomie-du-patient)
- [**Flag a lesion or a suspicious area** ROI on the slices, shown in 3D and at the MDT meeting.](@imagerie#lire-les-coupes)

</div>

## For the SEEG

<div class="cards" markdown>

- [**Propose an implantation scheme** From the hypotheses of the MDT meeting.](@seeg#planifier)
- [**Import the neurosurgeon's plan** ROSA, CSV or `electrodes.tsv`.](@seeg#planifier)
- [**Localise the electrodes on the CT** Automatic detection, checked one by one.](@seeg#localiser-les-electrodes)
- [**Mark seizure onset on the contacts** Electrical tab of the seizure.](@seeg#les-crises-seeg)
- [**Compute the epileptogenicity index** Recording, seizure window, EI in a few seconds.](@seeg#analyser-les-traces)
- [**Enter stimulations** Pair, intensity, response, in three clicks.](@seeg#stimulations)
- [**Plan thermocoagulations** Quick entry "Ia 1-3, OC 4-7".](@seeg#thermocoagulations)
- [**Write the SEEG report** Sections suggested from the data.](@seeg#le-rapport-seeg)

</div>
